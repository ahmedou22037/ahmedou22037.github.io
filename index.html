01 — About

Finance mind.
Data instinct.

I am a Finance graduate from Nouakchott Business School with a strong focus on credit risk modelling, banking systems, and data-driven financial analysis. My academic path began in mathematics and engineering preparatory classes before pivoting fully into finance — a background that shapes how I approach problems.

Over the past year, I have completed internships at both the Mauritania Investment Bank and the Central Bank of Mauritania, gaining direct exposure to commercial banking operations and monetary policy oversight. I have also developed two machine learning prototypes for credit scoring.

I am interested in the intersection of quantitative finance and emerging markets — particularly how modern credit risk frameworks like IFRS 9 and ECL can be applied in banking sectors that are still evolving.
